Rule of thumb: to keep loading error below 0.1%, R_load must be about 1000× the Thevenin resistance R1‖R2. A SAR ADC's input also needs charge at every sample. Put a capacitor (≈ 10–100 nF) from Vout to GND so it acts as a charge reservoir.
Worst case vs. typical: worst case assumes R1 at +tol and R2 at −tol (or vice versa). Statistically that's unlikely. See tools/tolerance_montecarlo.py for the distribution you'd actually see across a batch of boards.
