Transmission Line Reflections & Termination

A driver (voltage step behind a source resistance Rs) launches a wave into a 50 Ω line. At the far end, the load reflects a fraction ΓL = (RL−Z0)/(RL+Z0). Back at the source, Γs reflects it again. Press play to watch the wave, then try the presets.

Series termination: with R_s = Z0, only half the step (0.5 V) launches. The open load reflects +100%, doubling it to 1 V exactly at the receiver. The reflection returns to a matched source and is absorbed. The source pin shows a half-voltage "shelf" for one round trip, so this only works for loads at the end of the line.
Unterminated: a strong CMOS driver (≈15 Ω) plus an open end rings: overshoot ~50% above the rail, then ringing that decays over many round trips. With faster edges or an inductive load, the ringing can dip back through the logic threshold (false clocking). Increase the rise time to see why slow edges hide the problem on short traces.
