Decoupling Impedance Explorer

Every real capacitor is a series R-L-C. Put several in parallel and the network impedance |Z(f)| has valleys at each self-resonance and anti-resonance peaks between them. The PDN goal is to keep |Z| under the target impedance across the band where your chip draws current.

Try this: load the "classic" ladder and look at the peaks between the SRFs. Then load "same value": four identical caps lower both L and R without creating anti-resonances between themselves. Then load "long traces": the capacitance didn't change, but above ~20 MHz everything got worse. Mounted inductance is set by layout.
